Data sources & architecture

This is the part that turns the prototype into a production system.

Recommended production architecture

1. Search layer: provider-specific award/hotel inventory adapters normalize results into one schema.

2. Transfer layer: live partner matrix + transfer bonuses + transfer speed/availability rules.

3. Optimization engine: evaluates direct balances, one-hop transfers, and multi-bank funding; scores points required, cash taxes, redemption value, transfer friction, and opportunity cost.

4. Monitoring: scheduled searches store historical availability and trigger alerts when a saved itinerary crosses the user’s threshold.

5. User layer: goals, balances, companion travelers, preferred cabins, airport clusters, and booking rules.
